A PLACE TO STAY A MOMENT

Somewhere
between here
and the horizon.

Watch the last light leave the water.
Move your hand across it. Stay as long as you like.

SCROLL TO FOLLOW THE LIGHT
AN UNNAMED COAST
01 / THE HOUR BEFORE

The day lets go
slowly.

There is a moment when the sea holds every color at once.

02 / AFTER DARK

Nothing is
really still.

Even in the dark, the water keeps a little of the light.

03 / THE OTHER SIDE

Tomorrow arrives
without a sound.

Drag across the water. Let it answer.
